Privacy policy

The controller of your personal data is DD Volt [to be completed: full name, address, tax ID]. Contact: ddvoltinfo@gmail.com.

What data we collect

The data you provide in the contact form: full name, phone number, email address, company name and the content of your message. We do not collect data for any other purpose.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to a quote request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • For recruitment, when you send a job application (Article 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept until the conversation ends, for a maximum of 12 months. Job applications are kept until recruitment ends, unless you agree to a longer period.

Who we share it with

The form provider (Formspree) and our email provider. We do not sell data.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). To exercise these rights, write to the address above.
