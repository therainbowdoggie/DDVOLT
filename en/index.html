Frequently asked questions

Where do you operate?

We deliver industrial and automotive projects throughout Europe.

What documentation do you work from?

We work from electrical schematics, EPLAN, technical drawings and project documentation.

Do you only do electrical work?

No. We also provide mechanical assembly of production lines and industrial robots.

How quickly can you start a project?

We agree the start date individually, depending on the scope of work and team availability.

Do you hire people without experience?

Yes. We offer on-the-job training to people who want to develop in industrial electrical and mechanical work.

What form of cooperation do you offer?

An employment contract or B2B, whichever you prefer. With B2B we help with the paperwork at the start.

What are the assignments like?

Projects last from a few weeks to over a year. Accommodation and travel are agreed before you leave.

What are the requirements?

They depend on the role. Electricians need to know electrical schematics, fitters need experience in mechanical assembly. For helper roles we also take on people without experience.

Do I need to speak a foreign language?

Basic English or German helps, but it is not required for every role. The team leader handles communication on the project.

How do I apply?

Send an application through the contact form or call us. You can send your CV or a short note about your experience and availability.
