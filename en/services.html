From design to a working installation

We deliver electrical installations and assembly work for industry and the automotive sector. From cable routes to connecting machines, equipment and industrial robots.

Electrical

We install cable routes and cabling, and mount and connect industrial equipment in line with the technical documentation.

Scope of work

  • Reading schematics and EPLAN
  • Installing cable routes and cabling
  • Mounting and connecting control cabinets
  • Connecting machines, motors and sensors

Mechanical

We handle the mechanical assembly of production lines, install industrial robots and prepare equipment for start-up.

Scope of work

  • Installing industrial robots
  • Installing and positioning equipment
  • Mechanical assembly of production lines
  • Commissioning support

Facilities and buildings

We carry out electrical installations in industrial, commercial and public buildings.

Scope of work

  • Lighting and power installations
  • Switchboard installation
  • Cabling and cable routes
  • Electrical installations in buildings

Sectors we work in

  • Automotive

    Production lines, paint shops and robot cells. Projects for Tesla, Mercedes-Benz, Ford, BMW and Audi, among others.

  • Industry

    Bottling lines, industrial furnaces, and motor and equipment connections in production halls.

  • Buildings

    Data centres, schools, and commercial and public buildings.

From documentation to delivery.

  1. 1

    Documentation

    We review the technical documentation and agree the scope and schedule of the work.

  2. 2

    Team organisation

    We select the right crew and organise the work on site.

  3. 3

    Assembly and connections

    We carry out the electrical and mechanical installation in line with the technical documentation.

  4. 4

    Completion

    We check the completed installations and hand them over for the next stages of the project.
