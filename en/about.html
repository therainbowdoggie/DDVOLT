Electricians and mechanics with automotive experience.

DD Volt is a team with experience gained on production lines for Tesla, Mercedes-Benz, Ford, BMW and Audi. We deliver electrical and mechanical installation at industrial plants across Europe.

From Cologne to our own company.

It started with two years on the production line and in the paint shop at Ford in Cologne, as a subcontractor to SICA. Then came the Audi plant in Hungary, a Microsoft data centre and schools in Germany.

A year at Tesla Gigafactory was already under our own business. Later projects include a paint shop in Bergamo, a plant in Frankfurt, a BMW line in Munich, 130 robots installed in two weeks at the Mercedes-Benz plant in Kuppenheim, and projects in Poland.

Today we combine electrical and mechanical installation in one team, and we keep growing it.

How we work

  • Work in line with the documentation

    We carry out the work based on electrical schematics, EPLAN and technical documentation.

  • Clear arrangements

    We agree the scope, the schedule and the crew before work begins.

  • A leader on site

    On every project a team leader coordinates the crew and is responsible for organisation and day-to-day arrangements.

  • Transparent terms

    We agree the form of cooperation, accommodation and rates with our people before they travel.
