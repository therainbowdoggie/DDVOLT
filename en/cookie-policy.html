Cookie policy

The DD Volt website does not use cookies for tracking or analytics. It only stores in your browser what it needs to work.

What we store in your browser

  • dd-audience: the selected version of the content (for companies or for job seekers).
  • dd-theme: light or dark theme.
  • dd-form: a draft of an unsent form, so it is not lost when the page reloads. It is removed after sending.
  • dd-boot: a note that the intro animation has already been shown in this session.

This data stays in your browser (localStorage and sessionStorage). It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

External resources

Fonts, the map and all scripts are hosted together with the site. The site does not load anything from external servers.

No analytics

We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracking tools.
